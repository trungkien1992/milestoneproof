# BLI Legal Tech Hackathon 2: evidence and decision

Checked 7 October 2026. Primary pages were read directly, including DoraHacks' rendered tabs. This is an event-rules review and product hypothesis assessment; user interviews and pilots have not occurred.

## Decision

**Conditional GO for a capped BCH evidence assembly prototype and validation.** The submission is not yet confirmed eligible. Proceed with the useful, portable product work while requesting written rules; stop additional hackathon work if the organizer excludes solo Vietnam entrants, BCH, or disclosed reuse/AI assistance.

The job is a freelancer/bookkeeper handing a reviewer the records for one off-platform BCH milestone: which agreement version is being cited, what acceptance record was supplied, which invoice is associated, which unique outputs were reported, and what balance those records imply. The initial users the builder can reach are BCH freelancers and bookkeepers. There is no observed demand from them yet.

## Narrow MVP and limits

Implemented: local text sources with separate agreement-version IDs; manually entered milestone/invoice amounts and recipient; exact citations; operator-classified acceptance tied to a version; CSV/JSON BCH output claims; integer balances; no double credit; wrong-recipient/ambiguous/conflicting handling; written review decisions; full HTML/JSON packet and reproducible verifier; workspace save/import.

Deferred: OCR/PDF parsing, email/Drive connectors, fiat conversion, token quantities, chain queries, signing, anchoring, escrow, negotiation and legal recommendations. OCR and auto-extraction would add an accuracy problem before validating the handoff job. If manual transcription dominates effort, revise scope or stop.

Reusable core: BCH Close payment normalization/reconciliation at commit `8835382b306328b34912a3ff91fe61ed4168879d`. New work: source/version/citation model, report engine, acceptance/review UI, report formats/verifier, fixtures and hackathon materials. See [explicit provenance](REUSE.md).

## Effort, cost and stop conditions

Planning estimate for a solo builder: 12–18 focused hours for this prototype and preparation, then 6–10 hours recruiting, running and evaluating five short sessions and two pilots. These are estimates, not logged labor. Expected incremental prototype spending is $0–30 with existing ChatGPT access, GitHub and static hosting; hosting/account charges need checking before a paid commitment. No model API, wallet transaction or chain fee is needed. No purchase was made for this work.

Proceed to a credible submission when rules permit it, at least three of five reachable users show a recent recurring evidence problem, at least two provide consented/redacted cases and agree to a pilot, and the frozen correctness/time criteria in [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md) pass.

Stop or reposition if fewer than two users will try a real handoff, their current tool already does the job adequately, transcription dominates total effort, source references cannot be reviewed reliably, or eligibility excludes the implementation. Do not extend the build to chase awards while these questions are unanswered. The current evidence supports a prototype experiment, not a validated product claim.
