# Short validation interview: BCH freelancers and bookkeepers

Target five people reachable this week, ideally three freelancers and two bookkeepers with recent BCH project payments. One person can represent both roles; record that rather than inventing separate participants. Use pseudonymous participant IDs. Do not request wallet keys or unredacted client records.

## Message you can send

I'm testing a small tool that assembles an agreement version, milestone acceptance, invoice and BCH payment records into a reviewable evidence packet. Could I watch how you handled one recent project or bookkeeping handoff for about 10 minutes? Redacted or invented copies are fine for the first session. I'm trying to understand your current process and whether another tool would actually help. No payment or wallet connection is needed.

## Eight to ten minutes, before showing the prototype

1. Tell me about the last BCH-paid project or client handoff you completed. Who needed to check the records, and why?
2. Where were the agreement versions, acceptance messages, invoices and payment records? Please show your usual way of assembling them, using redacted records if needed.
3. What was hardest in that actual case? Did an amendment, partial payment, reused address, duplicated import or recipient mismatch cause a problem? Which one actually happened?
4. How much time did you spend collecting, checking and sharing the records? Which steps do you remember well enough to estimate? How often have you done this in the last three months?
5. How did the reviewer decide what was accepted and what remained unpaid? What evidence did they ask you to add or correct?
6. What does your current invoice/accounting/signature tool already cover? Would this packet duplicate that work?

Then show the fictional prototype. Ask them to locate the selected agreement, the acceptance record, the confirmed balance and the duplicated output. Watch without explaining the answer. Record confusion and errors as well as completion.

Close: Is there one upcoming or recent handoff we could try with consented, redacted records? Who could independently check the expected answer? Agree on a specific case and session only if they want to participate.

## Record, without turning opinions into outcomes

Participant role, last actual case, existing tools, record locations, observed task/error, recalled assembly time marked as an estimate, recent frequency, missing evidence, willingness to supply one case, and next agreed step. A positive opinion is not a pilot, willingness to pay, adoption or measured time saved.
